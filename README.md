# 🧠 Brain-Health-Risk-Analysis
Brain health risk analysis using, Excel, PowerBI, Power Query and DAX

---

## 📌 Overview

The Brain Tumor Distribution Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to provide a comprehensive overview of brain tumor patient data. It enables healthcare professionals, researchers, and analysts to monitor diagnosis trends, identify high-risk demographics, and evaluate tumor characteristics through dynamic visualisations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The dashboard transforms raw healthcare data into meaningful insights, supporting evidence-based decision-making and improving the understanding of brain tumor patterns.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyse the distribution of brain tumor cases.
- Identify the most affected age groups and brain regions.
- Monitor annual diagnosis trends.
- Compare confirmed and unconfirmed cases.
- Explore tumor types and their prevalence.
- Support healthcare planning with data-driven insights.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
**KPI Cards**
- Total Patients – Displays the total number of recorded patients.
- Average Age – Shows the average age of diagnosed patients.
- Average Tumor Size – Indicates the average tumour size across all patients.Average Intake Per Year – Compares yearly patient intake against the overall average.
- Most Affected Age Group – Highlights the age group with the highest number of cases.
- Most Affected Brain Region – Displays the brain region most frequently affected.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI – Dashboard development and data visualisation
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPIs, measures, and calculated columns
- Healthcare Dataset – Brain tumour patient records

---
